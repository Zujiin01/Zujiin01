## Work with your AI agent from your phone (free trilogy)

Three small projects that work alone and are better together. They are for people who use **Claude on mobile** or **Grok / GrokBot on mobile** and want their AI to act on **their own server**, with no computer and no SSH.

| # | Project | What it does |
|---|---------|--------------|
| 1 | [**The Door**](https://github.com/Zujiin01/mobile-server-connection) | An HTTPS API that lets your phone AI read files, run commands and manage services on your server. |
| 2 | [**OPS Memory**](https://github.com/Zujiin01/ops-memory) | A shared memory for your AIs: they search patterns of problems already solved instead of repeating mistakes. |
| 3 | [**The Bridge**](https://github.com/Zujiin01/the-bridge) | A folder where your AIs send each other messages and wake up the moment one arrives. Includes a web page behind a key to write to them from your phone. |

You need a server (Linux). Everything is public, tested, and free for **non-commercial** use ([PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://github.com/Zujiin01/the-bridge/blob/main/LICENSE)).
